Data Story • 2015–2025

San Francisco Crime Analysis

Exploring how crime patterns are shifting across districts, time, and behavior— with a focus on rising burglary trends in specific areas.

Key Insights

1. Overall crime is declining.

Crime has decreased compared to the pre-COVID baseline, indicating general improvement in safety trends.

2. Burglary is increasing in specific districts.

Despite the overall decline, burglary shows localized growth in certain areas—suggesting that crime is not evenly distributed.

3. Crime is becoming more patterned.

Burglary incidents are concentrated in specific time windows, revealing predictable behavioral patterns.

Key Visualizations

What This Means

Although San Francisco shows an overall decline in crime, the increase in burglary in specific districts reveals an important insight: crime patterns are becoming more localized rather than uniform.

This suggests that broad crime reduction strategies may not be enough. Instead, targeted interventions focused on high-risk areas and peak times could be more effective.
