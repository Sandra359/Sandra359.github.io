SF Crime Analysis - Assignment 2
This project explores how crime patterns in San Francisco shifted during the COVID-19 pandemic, with a focus on burglary from 2015 to 2025. Using SFPD incident data, it uncovers how changes in daily life reshaped where and when crime occurred across the city.
Final Project
An interactive data story on housing affordability in Denmark from 2008–2024. Through visualizations and narrative, it explores how rising prices, interest rates, and regional differences have reshaped who can afford to live where.
